Would you start over if you could? Would you leave everything behind and start again from scratch?

There's been moments where I would've answered yes. There's many things I would've done differently in my life if I had the chance to start over.

Wouldn't you shape to be kind of boring though? If personalities are shaped from lived experience, wouldn't someone who has done everything right shape into someone you wouldn't want to be around.

That's not who I want to become. Mistakes have shaped me, constantly running after perfection ruined me.

What about a new beginning? Moving to a country you've never been to before, a town you don't know much about and a job somewhat like what you've done before, but different enough for it to feel new.

Yes, I'd do that.

2 weeks ago I moved to the United States to work at [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com) in-person.

I don't think it's fully dawned on me yet how fucking crazy this is. Leaving pretty much my entire social life behind in the Netherlands, to start a new beginning here.

Is this forever? No, just a year, but a year's a long time (as short as it sometimes may seem).

But I'm excited for what's to come.
