## quick rundown

In case you aren't familiar with Pterodactyl, Blueprint modding framework, WISP, and Infraly LLC; here's a quick list for you with a short explaination of what they are:

- **Pterodactyl** is free and open-source software you can self-host that lets you manage a Minecraft server (or other kind of game-servers) through a nice web UI. It's used by many hosting providers.
- **Blueprint modding framework** is an **independent** toolkit used by developers and system administrators to install "extensions" directly to their Pterodactyl panel. Say you want to add a Minecraft player manager to your Pterodactyl dashboard for example, Blueprint let's you install such extensions.
- **WISP** is a closed-source SaaS that competes directly with Pterodactyl, promising better support and more features, that costs money.
- **Infraly LLC** owns WISP and more recently bought Pterodactyl, runs and maintains it.

## so what happened

Earlier today, there was an announcement in the Pterodactyl Discord community, [linking a YouTube Premiere](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ER4kv0jVEU). (hasn't aired yet as of writing this blog post)

People initially seemed somewhat positive, but also cautious, as Infraly, after all, is a for-profit company.

There were already rumors going around of a potential Pterodactyl v2 update, but also of a so-called Pterodactyl+ panel (and WISP being renamed to it). Nevertheless the entire "building in private" deal. The community wasn't happy about it.

Nothing of this was really addressed, and whenever asked, Anthony (from Infraly) would reply with a Discord meme GIF of some sorts or any other meme-y/generic reply.

Then, someone posted a publicly accessible link to the Pterodactyl+ billing instance (billing.wisp.infraly.co). People got pissed.

Someone else found a publicly accessible link to a Pterodactyl v2 instance (next.pterodactyl.io) and grabbed the React JS application bundle and got it working with the Pterodactyl v1 API. Immediately screenshots of the new Pterodactyl v2 panel started spreading around.
